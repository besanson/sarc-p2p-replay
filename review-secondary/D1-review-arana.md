Thanks for sending this. I read the packet, and these are the points I would clarify:

In the abstract, “every clearing” sounds broader than the group actually analysed after the exclusions.

* For H2, Section 5.2 compares D4 failures with D4 passes, but the conclusion calls the comparison group “clean clearings.” I would keep the same description in both places.
* The human and model results have different denominators. The table shows this, but I think the text should explain why some human observations are missing. Also, a block or delay in the log is not quite the same thing as asking a model to hold or clear.
* The D5 change is disclosed, but its effect on the model sample deserves a clearer explanation.
* I would explain “expected correctives” more directly: which rate is assigned to each defect group, and which cases receive no estimate.
* In the coverage table, D1′ says “final approval,” although the registered rule includes other approval events.

My first question as a skeptical reader would be whether the human/model comparison could be read as more directly comparable than it really is.

I appreciated that the draft reports the unsupported and inconclusive results clearly. These comments are about making the wording more precise, not asking for more experiments.
