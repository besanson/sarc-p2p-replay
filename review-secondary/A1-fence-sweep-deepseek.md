## The related-work sentence

The sentence holds for its first two clauses — conformance checking, the three-way match and rework analysis are correctly disclaimed as not introduced — and no complete antecedent was found for the third clause, so "puts people, models and the gate on the same lines" can stand as the contribution. One fix is warranted: "the evidence it relied on" overstates what the log can show. The paper reconstructs evidence from the event log at clearing time; it does not establish which information the original human approver actually consulted. The sentence should read "the evidence reconstructed from the event log at clearing time" (or equivalent), otherwise it asserts a fact about the original decision-maker the design cannot support.
