## Data

Verified on 2026-10-12 from the 4TU.ResearchData record pages and the 4TU v2
API (`/v2/articles/{id}`, `/v2/collections/{id}`), not from memory. Each file was
downloaded once into `data/` (gitignored), its MD5 checked against the MD5 that
4TU supplies and computes, and its SHA-256 computed locally. The only
inspection of the content was a count of opening `<trace` and `<event` tags
(`scripts/confirm_counts.py`), which matches the counts the record pages
publish. Nothing else was parsed. Machine-readable copy: `prereg/data.lock`.

### D.3 Redistribution

Both licences permit redistribution with attribution (BPI 2020: non-commercial
only). The logs are nevertheless not committed (size, and the NC term on the
replication logs): `data/` is gitignored and `make bootstrap` fetches each file
by its URL in `prereg/data.lock` and checks the SHA-256 above. See DECISIONS.md
D-A0-1.
