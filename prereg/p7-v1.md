## 1. Question and invariant

**Question.** How often did a real purchase-to-pay process clear an invoice on
evidence that fails one of the programme's own predicates, in count and in
value; what followed such clearings in the log; what would a deterministic
min() gate have held; and what do two model families decide on the same
evidence beside the people in the log and the gate?

**Invariant.** The gate is deterministic and reads only metadata reconstructed
from events recorded at or before the clearing time. No model enters the gate
or any primary estimate. E4 measures what a model would decide; it never
produces a verdict.

## 2. Window, observation end and frame (BPI 2019)

Terms: a **line** L is one case (purchase document and item). A **clearing** is
one `Clear Invoice` event on L at time t_c. "Before t_c" means timestamp less
than or equal to t_c, excluding the clearing event itself (equal timestamps
count as available; section 3.3).

- **Window.** Lines whose earliest `Create Purchase Order Item` timestamp lies
  in [2018-01-01T00:00:00Z, 2018-12-31T23:59:59Z]. The record page states time
  coverage 2018.
- **Observation end.** T_end is the latest `time:timestamp` of any event in the
  log, computed by the reader and reported as a slot.
- **Frame.** A line is in the frame if it is in the window, survives the
  exclusions below, and either (a) its first clearing satisfies
  t_c <= T_end - W, or (b) it has no clearing and its creation time is at most
  T_end - W. The analysed clearings are every clearing on a framed line with
  t_c <= T_end - W, so every outcome window is complete.
- **E0 lines.** The 500 E0 pilot lines (section 6, E0) are removed from the
  frame before any estimate.

### 2.1 Exclusions, applied in this order, each count a slot

1. Lines with no `Create Purchase Order Item` event in the log (truncated prefix).
2. Consignment lines (section 3.1).
3. Lines with zero or missing line value (section 3.4).
4. Lines with more than **K = 12** clearings in the log (framework agreements).
   K is the record's own example of an ordinary recurring line: rent, twelve
   receipts and twelve cleared invoices.
5. Lines outside the window, then lines failing the frame condition.

### 2.2 Stop conditions at E0 (re-register as p7-v1.1 before E1)

E1 does not start, and the registration is amended and re-tagged, if E0 shows
any of: fewer than 50% of all `Create Purchase Order Item` events in the window;
T_end after 2019-12-31T23:59:59Z (timestamp outliers make T_end meaningless); a
column in `prereg/column-mapping.yaml` absent from the log; the flag-derived
category (section 3.1) undefined for more than 1% of lines. An activity in
`prereg/outcome-patterns.yaml` that never occurs in the log is not a stop
condition: it is reported with a zero count and stays in the registered list.

### 2.3 Strata (reported beside, never modelled in)

Item category (section 3.1); spend area (`Spend area text`); company
(`Company`, anonymised id); month of t_c; line value quartile (quartiles over
framed lines); vendor activity quartile (quartiles of framed lines per
`Vendor`). Company and vendor ids appear only as stratum labels in machine
output, never in prose, tables or figures of the paper.

## 3. Evidence, predicates and contract (brief section 3)

### 3.1 Category

The contract is selected by the case flags, as the record states the rules:

| category | `GR-Based Inv. Verif.` | `Goods Receipt` | `Item Type` | contract |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3-way, invoice after receipt | true | true | any but consignment | D1 to D7 |
| 3-way, invoice before receipt | false | true | any but consignment | D1 to D7 (invoice may precede receipt; clearing must follow it) |
| 2-way | false | false | any but consignment | D3 to D7 (D1, D2 dropped) |
| consignment | (any) | (any) | consignment | excluded |

"Consignment" is matched case-insensitively in `Item Type` or `Item Category`.
The `Item Category` string is read as a cross-check; the count of lines where it
disagrees with the flag-derived category is a slot. A line with
`GR-Based Inv. Verif.` true and `Goods Receipt` false has no category in the
record's rules; it is reported and excluded under the stop condition in 2.2.

### 3.2 Predicates

Each predicate takes the value pass, fail or unknown. Unknown means a required
input is missing (for example an empty or `NONE` user). Activity lists are in
`prereg/outcome-patterns.yaml`; counts are over events on L before t_c.

| id | name | dimension | fails when (on L, before t_c) | applies to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | receipt_present | presence | #`Record Goods Receipt` - #`Cancel Goods Receipt` <= 0 | 3-way categories |
| D2 | receipt_before_clearing | freshness, order | no uncancelled receipt precedes t_c (same count as D1; see 3.3) | 3-way categories |
| D3 | invoice_present | presence | no `Record Invoice Receipt` | all |
| D4 | unchanged_since_approval | validity | a `Change Price` or `Change Quantity` with timestamp after the last approval-bearing event and at most t_c | all |
| D5 | distinct_actors | authority | the actor of `Clear Invoice` equals an actor of the earliest `Create Purchase Order Item`; unknown if either actor is empty or `NONE` | all |
| D6 | block_respected | scope | #`Set Payment Block` - #`Remove Payment Block` > 0 | all |
| D7 | not_deleted | validity | #`Delete Purchase Order Item` + #`Block Purchase Order Item` - #`Reactivate Purchase Order Item` > 0 | all |

Approval-bearing events (D4) are listed in `prereg/outcome-patterns.yaml`; the
earliest `Create Purchase Order Item` is always one, so D4 is defined on every
framed line.

### 3.3 D1 and D2, and the receipt defect class

At t_c, D1 and D2 have the same truth value on every clearing: both fail iff no
uncancelled receipt is recorded before t_c. They are kept as two predicates
because they carry two SARC-DQ dimensions; the gate verdict is the same either
way. For reporting only, a clearing failing D1/D2 is assigned to one defect
class by what the log records after t_c: **stale receipt** (D2 class) if an
uncancelled receipt is recorded on L after t_c and at most T_end; **absent
receipt** (D1 class) otherwise. The class never enters the gate.

Counts and "after the last approval-bearing event" are evaluated on timestamps,
so the verdict is invariant under reordering of events with equal timestamps
(property test, Phase B).

### 3.4 Values

- Line value: the `Cumulative net worth (EUR)` value on the earliest
  `Create Purchase Order Item` event.
- Cleared value of a clearing: the value on the `Clear Invoice` event; if missing
  or not positive, the value on the latest `Record Invoice Receipt` on L before
  t_c; if that is missing too, the line value. Each fallback count is a slot.
- Values are in the record's anonymised euros (linear translation respecting
  0). The paper reports them as "anonymised EUR".

### 3.5 Contract and verdict

The contract of a clearing is the predicate set of its category (3.1). Verdict
**allow** iff every applicable predicate passes; otherwise the gate **would have
held** it. This is min() over the set with pass = 1 and fail = unknown = 0. A
clearing is **defective** if at least one applicable predicate fails; a clearing
whose only non-pass value is unknown is held but not defective, and is counted
separately. "Defect" is used only for a failed predicate.

### 3.6 Human hold

A line is **held by people** if a `Set Payment Block` is recorded on L before its
first clearing, or if no clearing is recorded within W days after its first
`Record Invoice Receipt`. Lines with no invoice receipt by T_end - W are not
classified (count is a slot).

### 3.7 Outcome proxy O

O = 1 for a clearing if any activity in the `outcome` list of
`prereg/outcome-patterns.yaml` is recorded on L with timestamp in
(t_c, t_c + W]. **W = 60 days**, primary. Sensitivity at **W = 30 days**,
descriptive only (the frame stays the W = 60 frame). Outcome activities:
`Cancel Invoice Receipt`, `Cancel Goods Receipt`, `Change Price`,
`Change Quantity`, `Delete Purchase Order Item`, `Vendor creates debit memo`,
`Record Subsequent Invoice`.

### 3.8 Lag bands (D1/D2 failures)

Time from t_c to the first uncancelled `Record Goods Receipt` on L after t_c,
in days: **[0, 1]**, **(1, 7]**, **(7, 30]**, **over 30**, and **never** (no
receipt by T_end). Reported per band in count and cleared value.

### 3.9 Evidence set

One record per analysed clearing: line id (hashed in any export), category,
t_c, predicate inputs, each predicate's value, verdict, defect classes, O at
W = 60 and W = 30, cleared value, line value, and the SHA-256 of the canonical
serialisation of the source events on L up to t_c. A 1,000-record sample
(seeded) is exported to `results/`.

## 4. Hypotheses

One family for Holm at alpha = 0.05: H1, H2, H3, H4-Jev, H4-Haiku (five
tests). Everything else is descriptive.

| id | claim | groups (analysed clearings) | test |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | C3 | defect: 3-way clearings with D1/D2 fail. Comparison: 3-way clearings with every applicable predicate passing. | one-sided, O rate difference > 0 |
| H2 | C4 | D4 fail vs D4 pass, all categories | one-sided, O rate difference > 0 |
| H3 | C5 | D5 fail vs D5 pass (unknown excluded, count reported), all categories | one-sided, O rate difference > 0 |
| H4 | C6 | per family: hold rate on defective lines vs on clean lines (E4) | two-sided equivalence (TOST), margin m = 0.05 |

**H1 to H3 statistic.** Difference in O rate, defect group minus comparison
group, at W = 60. Standard error from a bootstrap that resamples purchase
documents (clusters) with replacement, B = 10,000, seed 20261201;
z = difference / SE; one-sided p = 1 - Phi(z). The H1 comparison group is
restricted to 3-way categories so that D1/D2 apply to both groups; the
comparison against all-pass clearings of every category is reported beside it,
descriptively.

**H4 statistic.** Per family, d = hold rate on defective sample lines minus hold
rate on clean sample lines. Two one-sided z-tests with unpooled standard error
against -m and +m, m = 0.05; p_TOST = max of the two. Equivalence (Flat Ladder)
is supported if the Holm-adjusted p_TOST < 0.05. The sample draws at most one
line per purchase document, so lines are treated as independent.

## 5. Intervals and inference conventions

Shares of clearings: Wilson 95% interval and, beside it, the purchase-document
clustered bootstrap percentile interval (B = 10,000, seed 20261201). Shares of
value: clustered bootstrap only. All bootstraps use one `numpy.random.Generator`
(PCG64) seeded 20261201, resampling purchase documents in sorted id order.

## 7. E4: agent replay

- **Sample.** Framed lines (E0 lines removed) with at least one
  `Record Invoice Receipt`. Decision point t*: the line's first clearing if one
  is recorded within W days of its first invoice receipt, else first invoice
  receipt + W days. A line is **defective** if the gate verdict at t* has at
  least one failed predicate, **clean** if every applicable predicate passes;
  held-unknown lines are not sampled. N_d = **1,000** defective and
  N_c = **1,000** clean lines, at most one per purchase document, stratified by
  category x value quartile with proportional allocation (largest remainder),
  drawn with `random.Random(20261201)` over sorted line ids. The same 2,000
  lines go to both families. The draw is committed to `results/` before any
  call.
- **View.** The ERP view at t*, serialised by `prereg/erp-view-template.txt`.
  No event timeline. Value fields are anonymised EUR; company and vendor fields
  are not shown.
- **Families, as paper 6.** (1) TypeSafe System One (Jev), `typesafe-sdk==0.7.2`,
  endpoint `https://api.typesafe.ai/v1/systemone`; (2) Anthropic Messages API,
  model `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`, temperature 0, max_tokens 48, no system
  prompt, assistant prefill `{`. The returned model string is checked; a change
  stops the run (`MODEL_VERSION_CHANGED`).
- **Task.** Clear or hold, with a probability, as the template states. Parser
  as paper 6 jev-v3 section 3b, with fields `decision` in {`clear`, `hold`} and
  `p_hold` in [0, 1]. An invalid reply is re-requested once, then scored
  `invalid`, and counts as neither hold nor clear; invalid counts are slots.
- **Arm health rule (hard stops, as paper 6 jev-v3 3c).** Per family: the first
  20 sample lines in registered order run first; more than 2 invalid stops the
  family `ARM_INVALID`. After the first 200 lines, more than 5% invalid (more
  than 10) stops the family `ARM_INVALID` before the full sample. Stops are
  logged in `responses/manifest-e4.json` and are not resumable.
- **Cap.** USD 60 across E4 and the exploratory arm, retries included,
  enforced with paper 6's pre-call worst-case bound. A cap stop is
  `CAP_TRUNCATED`; H4 is then reported on the completed lines and marked
  truncated.
- **Exploratory, labelled.** E4 with the full event timeline up to t* instead
  of the ERP view, same lines, same families, run only after both primary arms
  finish and within the remaining cap. Never in the Holm family.
- Raw responses are committed append-only to `responses/`.

## 8. E7: replication on BPI 2020 declarations

Unit: each payment event (`payment` list in `prereg/outcome-patterns.yaml`) on
a declaration case, in either declaration log. Frame: payment at most
T_end' - W with T_end' the latest timestamp in that log; W = 60.

| id | name | fails when (before the payment) |
|---|---|---|
| D1' | approval_present | no event in the `approval` list |
| D2' | approval_by_required_role | no event in the `final_approval_required_role` list |
| D3' | unchanged_since_approval | an event in the `resubmission` list after the last approval |

O' = 1 if any event in the `outcome_prime` list (rejection or resubmission) is
recorded on the case in (t_pay, t_pay + W]. The BPI 2020 activity vocabulary is
registered in `prereg/outcome-patterns.yaml` from the challenge documentation
and checked at C0; an absent activity is reported, not replaced.
